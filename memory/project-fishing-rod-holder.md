Brian's garage has two wooden combs (1x4 on edge, screwed to the side face of exposed 2x8-looking joists, 27 in long, 20 in hang, half-round 1-1/4 in notches at about 5 in) holding rods horizontally above the open garage door. Rods stay at rest but any bump while pulling a neighbour drops one: a half-round in a vertical edge has its low point at the board edge.

Decided 2026-11-07: "use the deep hook design", and for the build "make individual deep hooks, that can be screwed into a 1x4 piece of wood" (a blend of the printed comb and the caps-on-wood option). Built the same day: `projects/Fishing-rod-holder/rod_hook.py`, one hook 3-1/2 x 3-1/4 x 3/8 in, two #8 x 1 screws 1 in from the board edge, reaches 1-3/4 past the edge so the rod lies beside the wood; pocket 7/8, lip 3/4, lift to leave 1.00 in for 1/4 to 5/8 blanks (pose-space check in the script). PETG 2 walls 15%: 24.6 g / 33 min per hook. Board layout drawn at 4 in pitch (5 in if reels stay on). Options page https://claude.ai/artifact/MuJqbgWZjUNzqSTMLFRt4H carries the Decided banner, the built section and the concept round. Not printed; rod count, reels and comb spacing still unknown but only set how many hooks and the pitch.

**How to apply:** after the print, record the real blank fit and the feel of the 1 in lift in `knowledge/learnings/fishing-rod-holder.md`; a mirrored hook is one flag away if a board needs both edges.
