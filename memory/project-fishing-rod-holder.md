Status 2026-11-07: options page published (https://claude.ai/artifact/MuJqbgWZjUNzqSTMLFRt4H), nothing built. Unknowns folded into the drawings: rod count, reels on or off (5 in pitch if on and in line), comb spacing, joist size, mouth direction.
