A figure-8 fastener recess in a rail, apron or cleat is bored off-center: with a 5/8 in forstner bit, center it about 1/4 in from the INNER face (the side the top overhangs into), so the bore opens about 1/16 in through that face. The fastener's free loop then hangs over the edge and the whole fastener lies flush against the underside of the top. Never center the bore on the edge.

**Why:** On 2026-09-27 the Drawer-bench model had the recesses centered across the 3/4 in top edge; Brian corrected it: "they should be off center so the figure 8 fastener can hang off and sit flush." Centered, the fastener's waist rides on the sliver of wood between the bore and the inner face and holds the top off.

**How to apply:** Any furniture model with figure-8 fasteners ([[project-drawer-bench]], Cabinet-bench, tables): position the recess from the inner face, keep a solid wall on the outer face (the bore must not break the show side or a glued face), and put the offset and the "opens through the inner face" wording on the cut-list note. The volume identity uses the partial-cylinder volume (circle minus the segment past the face).
