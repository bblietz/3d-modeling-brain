The client changed the alcove bench to a freestanding piece with room for the Roomba under it on 2026-10-26. It lives in `projects/Freestanding-bench/` (`freestanding_bench.py`, same conventions as the built-in model) and the built-in design in [[project-built-in-bench]] stays untouched in case they go back. Everything above the floor gap carries over (14 in top, 17 in seat, white frame-and-panel fronts, brass pulls, maple top 3 in back from the pilasters, Blum 563H undermounts, Baltic birch boxes, Osmo). New: 5 in of clear floor (tallest Roomba 3.9 in, rug margin), so the drawer boxes are 6-1/2 in instead of 8-1/2.

Legs: the drawer bench's construction ([[project-drawer-bench]]), Brian's pick A (2-1/2 in square) from five companion options on 2026-10-26 after my legs-under-a-box first pass was rejected: proud soft maple corner posts floor to top, 3/8 chamfer full length, the painted 3/4 ply ends and the 1/2 ply back housed in stopped grooves 1/2 in behind the post faces with notched bottom corners, the fronts between the front posts 1/4 in behind their faces, the case bottom in rabbets notched around the posts, a rear cleat for the top's figure-8s, Blum rear brackets on the post side.

Version 3 (Brian 2026-11-01: "remove the notch in the back of the legs, increase the overhang of the top such that it has 1/4 in around the edges from the wall"): the baseboard notch is gone, the rear posts stand 2 in off the back wall (3/4 in clear of the 1-1/4 in baseboard estimate), the top is 65-1/2 x 24-3/4 at 1/4 in from the back and both side walls, overhanging the posts 1-1/2 at the ends, 3/4 at the front and 1-3/4 at the back (the posts stayed put, END_OVERHANG is its own constant). Posts off the baseboard cost 1-3/4 in of case depth, so Brian picked 18 in Blum 563H4570B (over pulling the case forward or the baseboard out): inside depth 516.1 mm against 481, and the 2 in is set by the rear bracket (471 mm measured runner body, tail must be within 48 mm of the back; 1-3/4 in left 51 mm, 2 in leaves 44.6).

Status 2026-11-01: all asserts pass; cut list, STEP, 15 drawing sheets, hero, assembly images, shop page https://claude.ai/artifact/K37nFPcZU75QxRSvEAmfLL (PDF) republished for Version 3; options page https://claude.ai/artifact/QLCHadrNJZDeDjqka9yond untouched (Version 2, posts decided). `images/bench-in-nook-dogs.png` is still the Version 1 picture (notched posts, 64 in top), chain not rerun (about $0.50). Open: site measurements (opening at every height for the 65-1/2 top, baseboard and shoe under 2 in off the back wall and 1-3/4 off the side walls), the receptacle behind the case, the hook-bore scrap test, Brian's look at STILE_W, whether to rerun the room picture.
