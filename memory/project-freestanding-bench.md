The client changed the alcove bench to a freestanding piece with room for the Roomba under it on 2026-10-26. It lives in `projects/Freestanding-bench/` (`freestanding_bench.py`, same conventions as the built-in model) and the built-in design in [[project-built-in-bench]] stays untouched in case they go back. Everything above the floor gap carries over (14 in top, 17 in seat, white frame-and-panel fronts, brass pulls, maple top, Blum 563H 21 in, Baltic birch boxes, Osmo). New: 5 in of clear floor (tallest Roomba 3.9 in, rug margin), so the drawer boxes are 6-1/2 in instead of 8-1/2; top 64 x 24-3/4 with 1 in of air to each wall and 1/4 in to the back wall.

How it stands: my first version (four short legs under a box, blades or gables as alternatives) was wrong; Brian: "look at the legs for the other bench we designed and let's start there, show 5 options, visual companion". The legs are now the drawer bench's construction ([[project-drawer-bench]]): proud solid maple corner posts floor to top, 3/8 chamfer full length, the painted 3/4 ply ends and the 1/2 ply back housed in stopped grooves 1/2 in behind the post faces with notched bottom corners, the fronts between the front posts 1/4 in behind their faces, the case bottom in rabbets notched around the posts, a rear cleat for the top's figure-8s, Blum rear brackets on the post side. Brian picked A, 2-1/2 in square, from A 2-1/2, B 3 (the drawer bench's), C 3-1/2, D stopped chamfer over a square foot, E tapered foot (`leg_options.py`, shown in the superpowers visual companion). The rear posts are notched 1-1/2 x 4-1/2 over the alcove's baseboard (BASEBOARD_NOTCH=0 if it comes out); that notch, the 1/4 in wall gap and the 1/2 in back are what keep the 21 in runners' 557 mm behind fronts held 3 in back from the pilasters.

Status 2026-10-26: all asserts pass; cut list, drawings, hero, assembly images, shop page https://claude.ai/artifact/K37nFPcZU75QxRSvEAmfLL (PDF) and the options page https://claude.ai/artifact/QLCHadrNJZDeDjqka9yond (Version 2, decided banner, the five posts) are published; `images/bench-in-nook-dogs.png` is the checked sign-off picture (guided $0.166, styled $0.168, dogs $0.164; a one-pass styled with the dog photos rebuilt the scene as the concept picture, $0.172 wasted). Open: site measurements (opening at every height, baseboard and shoe under 1-1/2 in off the wall), the receptacle behind the case, the hook-bore scrap test, the 563H 21 in runner body length (547 assumed), Brian's look at STILE_W.

**Why:** CLEAR 5 in, the 1/4 in wall gap, the 1/2 back, the notch and the drawer bench's setbacks are my calls; the post size is Brian's; the drawers losing 2 in is the cost of the clearance at a fixed seat height.
