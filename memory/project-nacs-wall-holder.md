Wall-mounted dock for Brian's Tesla Gen 3 Wall Connector charge handle: drum on a 4 in square
plate, round flange with the Tesla T, wand out the drum's side hanging on a fixed cleat in the
connector's own lock pocket. **DONE 2026-09-19**: Brian mounted the final print and reports
"charger holder is on the wall and working great." Project files, tools and full history:
`projects/NACS-wall-holder/` (brief.md, `holder.scad`, `pipeline/insertion.py` for the
docking/hold check, `pipeline/rim_round.py` for the 1/16 in rim roundover). Page:
https://claude.ai/artifact/TzbzpN4voPdJoeMWF8jHRw. See [[feedback-check-docking-and-hold-kinematics]]
for the docking-motion technique this project produced.
