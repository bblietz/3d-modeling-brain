name: feedback-hardwood-grain-wraps
description: Never design a hardwood cabinet whose shell grain runs front to back; grain wraps around the box so the panels move together
metadata:
  type: feedback
---

Never design a hardwood cabinet with the shell grain running front to back. The grain wraps around the box: left to right on the top and bottom, vertical on the sides. The corner joints (finger or dovetail) are then cut in end grain, and all four panels move along the depth axis together.

**Why:** Brian, 2026-09-13, reviewing the first live speaker-cab order (walnut 1x12): front-to-back grain "will cause a disaster when the wood moves". The corners land in long grain and the panels move in different directions. The speaker-cab generator and construction note had printed "grain front to back on all four panels", which contradicted their own finger-joint geometry and movement figures.

**How to apply:** for every hardwood carcass (speaker cabs, furniture cabinets, boxes), specify wrap-around grain in the plan, cut list, and check table, and flag any tool or note that says front to back. Anything glued flat across a solid-wood panel crosses this grain: Brian ruled that hardwood speaker-cab shells get no glued stiffeners at all.
