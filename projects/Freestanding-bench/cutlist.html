Assembly10 steps, in order

  1. Posts

    Four 2-1/2 square soft maple posts, 13-1/4 long, each glued up from four 3/4 layers of 1x6 (2-5/8 x 14-1/4 blanks) and planed square, glue lines on the side faces (front and back show face grain). Chamfer the four vertical edges 3/8, full length; no grooves, the panels butt the posts. No notch: the rear posts stand 2 off the back wall, 3/4 clear of the baseboard and shoe. Drill the front posts' Blum runner screw holes on the inner face, 1-1/4 and 2-5/16 behind the front face. Before any glue-up, drill every Kreg pocket for 3/4 stock: ends 1-1/2 and 5-1/2 up at both vertical edges, back 3 and 5-1/4 up at both ends, partition 2-1/2 and 4-1/4 up at the rear edge, the bottom's underside along both end edges and the rear edge, all on the inside or underside faces; plus the top-attachment pockets along the top edges of the ends and partition near the front.

  2. End assemblies

    Clamp a 1/2 spacer to each post face so the end panel's outer face lands 1/2 behind it, then glue and pocket-screw each end to its front and rear post (1-1/4 fine-thread screws), top edge flush with the post tops, show face out, pockets inside. Check that each end is square and both posts stand plumb before the glue sets.

  3. Back and cleat

    Join the two end assemblies with the 3/4 back between the rear posts' inner faces, the same 1/2 spacer behind it, top edge flush, glued and pocket-screwed from inside (pockets above the Blum rear-bracket line): with no wall screws it is what keeps the case square. Glue the cleat inside along the back's top edge between the rear posts, over nothing (the back's pockets stop below it), and screw it from the inside (#8 x 1, countersunk, nothing through the back).

  4. Bottom

    With the case upside down on the bench, lower the bottom in between the ends and against the back, its corner notches around the four posts, its underside flush with the panels' bottom edges. Glue and pocket-screw it from the underside into the ends (3 a side) and the back (6), coarse-thread screws. Its underside is the ceiling of the 5 in of clear floor.

  5. Partition

    Turn the case upright. Stand the partition on the bottom, centered, against the back, with its notch at the top rear around the cleat and its front edge on the fronts' back-face line, 31/32 behind the post faces. Glue it, pocket-screw it to the back, then turn the case over again and drive 3 #8 x 2 countersunk screws up through the bottom into its edge (pre-drill on the center line).

  6. Slides and brackets

    Post-side runners: front screws into the posts' inner faces (#6 x 5/8), rears on Blum brackets screwed to the back with #6 x 1/2. Partition-side runners: screwed to the partition the frameless way. All four level on the case bottom, fronts 1/16 behind the fronts' back-face line.

  7. Drawer boxes

    Glue each 1/2 Baltic birch box up around its bottom, 6-1/2 in tall: front and back in the side rabbets, bottom in the side and front grooves with its underside 1/2 above the side edges, butted to the back, glued and screwed through the back with 4 #6 x 1 (pre-drill 3/32 into the bottom's edge, countersink). Notches and hook bores at the rear (drill scrap first: the bore leaves a thin skin). Clip the locking devices under the bottom at the front corners with #6 x 1/2 screws and set the boxes on the runners.

  8. Fronts and pulls

    Glue up each 1/2 plywood panel in its poplar frame, back faces flush so the panel sits 1/4 below the frame (groove every piece first, then cut the tongues and tenons to fit). Prime and paint the fronts and the ends white. Hang the fronts between the front posts: 1/8 to each post, 1/8 between them, 1/8 under the top, bottoms flush with the case underside, faces 1/4 behind the post faces, screwed from inside the box through oversize holes. Center the pulls.

  9. Top

    Set the 65-1/2 x 24-3/4 top overhanging the posts 1-1/2 at each end, 3/4 at the front and 1-3/4 at the back. Pocket screws up into it through the ends and partition near the front; figure-8s on the cleat at the back so it can move. Osmo Natural on the top and the posts.

  10. Cushion

    Cushion on. Slide the bench into the alcove with the top 1/4 off the back wall and 1/4 off each side wall, the rear posts clear of the baseboard. Done.
