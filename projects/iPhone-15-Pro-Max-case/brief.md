# iPhone 15 Pro Max case (TPU 95A HF)

A slim, form-fitting case for Brian's iPhone 15 Pro Max with open button windows, printed in Bambu TPU 95A HF on the X2D, with a PETG camera guard ring that snaps into it. First TPU project in the vault ([[tpu-x2d]], [[printer-x2d]]). Retrospective: [[iphone-15-pro-max-case]].

## Decisions already locked (Brian, 2026-09-20)

- Phone: iPhone 15 Pro Max (Brian wrote "iPhone 15 Max Pro"; there is no other 15 Max model).
- Wall thickness: 1.5 mm. The original ask said "thin ~5mm"; Brian confirmed 1.5 mm when asked.
- Buttons: NO button covers. Action button, volume up, volume down and the side button get open cutout windows ("These should be cutout holes").
- Nozzle: 0.4 mm on the X2D.
- Filament: Bambu TPU 95A HF, stock preset `Bambu TPU 95A HF @BBL X2D 0.4 nozzle`.
- Camera guard: REQUIRED ("make sure it has a camera guard built in"). The lenses stand 4.07 mm above the back glass, so the guard stands 3.4 mm proud of the 1.6 mm back; with the back on the bed that would be below the bed plane. Brian chose, from three options: TWO PIECES, a guard ring printed flat and bonded on with flexible CA glue (the glue was superseded later the same day, see "Guard material and fixing" below; two pieces stands). Rejected: one piece printed screen-side down on TPU supports (double time, rough inside floor); one piece back-down with the whole outside back on supports (rough visible back). Printing on edge is out: a 160 mm tall 1.5 mm TPU channel deflects about 15 mm under nozzle drag.
- Guard fit: SNUG around the camera. Brian, on seeing the first ring in the viewer: "the camera on the phone is correct, but the guard around it is too big. It should be snug around the camera." The first ring followed Apple's "plateau outer boundary", which is the base of the glass ramp, 4 mm off the raised island.
- Guard shape: SLIM, PLAIN, BEVELLED INSIDE. On the second ring (snug, 5.1 mm wide, with Apple's flash and LiDAR keepout cones cut out of it as two dished cutaways): "the ring is too thick and there are cutouts in the top right and bottom right. Not good", then "the guard should also be a bevel on the inside". So no cutaways: the ring crosses Apple's flash-outer and rear-sensor cones, as most commercial cases do (told to Brian: possible slight flash haze with light TPU; dark TPU avoids it).
- Inside of the case: NO bevel around the camera. On the third ring (the case floor sloped in over the glass ramp to carry the snug ring): "the inside of the case, around the camera should not have a bevel, only the guard has a bevel". So the case has a plain square-edged cutout and the ring carries a square plug that drops into it.
- MagSafe: "add a magsafe ring so I can insert the metal pieces"; "the ring is the standard size. .4mm thick".
- Guard material and fixing: "the guard should be petg and snap into place." So the ring is rigid PETG, there is no glue, and the TPU case does the flexing. Supersedes the flexible CA bond; everything about the ring's outside shape stands.
- Print file: "The 3mf file should be 2 plates, one for the guard and one for the case".

## Design as built (mine unless marked; all numbers are constants at the top of the source)

- Backend: build123d, canonical source `iphone-15-pro-max-case.py`. Every phone dimension is from Apple's dimensional drawing (`reference/iphone-15-pro-max-dimensions.md`): body 76.73 x 159.86 x 8.25, spline corners traced through Apple's ordinate points, Apple's edge cross-section.
- Case 79.93 x 163.06 x 10.8 mm, 23.8 cm3. Side walls 1.5, back 1.6 (8 layers), cavity 0.10 larger than the phone per side (printed TPU cavities come out a little small, and a case should grip).
- Screen lip: a 45 degree underside that leans in over the phone (a hook, checked in `images/section-lip.png`), 0.05 mm off the phone's shoulder at the point where Apple's edge profile is also at 45 degrees. Tip 0.85 mm in from the housing edge (glass starts at 1.00; Apple's drawing says do not touch the glass). Stands 0.95 mm proud of the screen (Apple: 0.85 min, 1.00 ideal).
- Windows, all centred on the band's mid plane: Action 8.04 long, volume up and down share one 27.4 long window (the buttons are only 3.0 apart), side button 19.7 long; each 5.0 tall inside, flaring 45 degrees to 6.5 outside, top and bottom only. Bottom: USB-C 13.0 x 7.0 (Apple asks 12.45 x 6.60 plus margin) and two acoustic slots 2.0 mm clear of the outermost holes (Apple's thin-case rule). Posts: 2.93 between Action and volume, 2.02 each side of USB-C.
- Camera opening: a plain square-edged cutout, 0.4 mm outside the base of the glass ramp that surrounds the camera island (Apple's plateau outer boundary, 44.18 x 45.50 with spline corners). Nothing inside the case is bevelled. It runs to within 1.04 mm of the top and side-button edges, so a 0.74 mm ledge of floor is left there. Buried in the cutout's wall is the groove for the ring's barb: 0.5 deep, from 0.6 above the outside of the back up to a 45 degree roof that closes at the floor's surface (the case prints back down, so the roof may not be flat). From inside the case only the plain hole shows (`images/render-inside.png`). Along the top and side-button edges the groove is cut into the root of the wall, with 1.7 mm of TPU left outside it (`images/section-snap-top.png`).
- Guard ring (fifth version, PETG, snap-in): a rim and a plug. The rim is the fourth version's: 3.0 mm wide, 3.4 mm tall, 1.5 mm 45 degree bevel on its inside, 0.5 chamfer on its outside top edge; inside wall 1.56 mm off the island's flat top; top 5.0 mm above the back glass, so the lens glass (4.07) is 0.93 mm off a table. It overlaps the case's back by 0.5 mm all round the cutout (the stop when the ring is pushed toward the phone). The plug is 1.2 mm tall (6 layers): a neck 0.05 smaller than the cutout all round, and above it a barb 0.45 proud of the neck, so it hooks 0.40 mm over the flap, the 0.6 mm (3 layers) of back left under the groove. The flap stretches over the barb's 45 degree lead-in on the way in and closes behind it; it then sits captive in the ring's own groove between the rim's land and the barb. Both holding faces are flat and square to the pull (no cam-out). The ring prints rim face down, so the holding face is an overhang: it is built as two steps on two layers (0.2, then 0.25), checked at the slicer's mid-layer planes, so each wall line is half carried by the layer below; a 45 degree root would have been cut by the slicer into 0.10 then 0.35. The plug's inside follows the camera's glass ramp, 0.4 mm (horizontal) off my straight-ramp proxy; its top is 0.91 wide. The model asserts, on the real solids: seated ring touches nothing; pulled out, it meets the flap (10 mm3 at 0.3, 22 mm3 at 0.5); pushed in, the land meets the back; on the way in it meets the flap and nothing above it. It still asserts Apple's three camera cones and the flash inner cone against the ring, and reports the accepted crossings (flash outer 3.4 mm3, rear sensor 58.3 mm3).
- MagSafe pocket: in the phone side of the back, so the phone holds the pieces in and nothing shows. Brian's ring is the standard size, 0.4 mm thick. Position and the alignment piece follow Apple's case array (ADG R30 Fig 42-2 to 42-4; `reference/iphone-15-pro-max-dimensions.md` section 16): ring centred on the product centre (the model origin) within 0.30, 46.00 ID; clocking magnet 6.00 x 19.31 from 31.18 to 50.49 below the ring centre toward the bottom edge. "Standard" metal rings on sale run 54 to 56 mm OD (Apple's own is 54.10) with the same 46 ID, so the pocket locates the ring by its inside edge (45.5) and takes any OD up to 56.5 (pocket 57.0). Depth 0.8 (4 layers), deeper than the 0.4 ring needs on purpose: the ring sits 0.4 below the floor's surface, off the phone's glass (Apple: no metal contact with the product), and 0.8 mm (4 layers) of back stays between the ring and the accessory (Apple allows at most 0.85). 4.0 mm clear of the guard ring. Leave the alignment pocket empty if the kit has no alignment piece. Constants `MAG_*` at the top of the source.
- Print orientation: case back on the bed, ring rim face on the bed (plug and barb up), no supports on either. The ring's visible face is its first layer, so it takes the textured plate's finish, like the back of the case. Overhangs: the six window roofs (68 mm2 of bridge in all), the rounded window ends, the groove's 45 degree roof, and the barb's two steps.

## Open risks for the first print

- The volume window's roof is a 22 mm TPU bridge (side button 15 mm, speaker slot 11 mm). The wall lines run along the bridge, the best case, but some sag is likely; it is cosmetic.
- Fit is unproven: cavity clearance 0.10 per side and the lip's 0.05 gap are my first guesses for TPU 95A HF.
- The snap is unproven: there is no measured snap-fit data in the vault in any material, and none for PETG in TPU. First guesses: neck 0.05 smaller than the cutout, barb 0.40 over a 0.6 x 0.5 mm flap. Too tight to get in, or too easy to pop out, is one number (`SNAP_BARB`) and an 11 minute reprint of the ring alone.
- The barb's holding face is a PETG overhang of two steps (0.2 and 0.25 mm) with no support. If the steps droop, the ring's groove closes up a little on the 0.6 mm flap (0.2 of play is left for that).
- The groove's 45 degree roof and the 0.6 mm flap are TPU details of two to three layers at the cutout's edge; first-layer squish on the flap's hole makes the neck fit tighter (TPU gives).
- Rigid PETG now sits 0.2 mm (normal) off my straight-ramp proxy of the camera glass. The real ramp is a concave fillet (not dimensioned by Apple) and sits lower. Pushed toward the phone, the ring travels 0.1 mm before the barb rests on the groove's TPU roof, which the phone backs; it needs 0.22 to reach the proxy.
- Peeling the case off the phone flexes the back around a rigid ring: start at a bottom corner. If the ring pops out, snap it back in.
- MagSafe ring OD not measured ("standard size"); the pocket takes 54 to 56.5. A steel attach ring (0.4 mm suggests steel, not magnets) is outside Apple's rules (permeability 1.05 max) but common on DIY cases; functional risk only (charging heat, compass).
- The ring crosses Apple's flash-outer and LiDAR cones (Brian's call). Use an opaque PETG; watch for haze in flash photos on the first print, a dark colour is the fix.

## Snapping the ring in

Phone out. From the outside of the back, hook the barb into the cutout along the top edge and the side-button edge first (there the case cannot stretch: the walls are right behind the cutout). Then work round the other two edges with a thumb, a finger inside the case easing the edge of the hole over the barb, like a button through a buttonhole. Home when the rim sits flat on the back all round; the cutout is 1.3 mm taller than wide, so it only goes in one way. Out again: phone out, fold the back away from the ring along a free edge and push the plug out from inside.

## Printing notes

TPU 95A HF is not an AMS filament: feed from the external spool, and dry it first. The PETG for plate 2 can come from the AMS. Brian slices and sends from Bambu Studio, one plate at a time; the GUI opening the 3MF is ground truth for the plates and the settings.
