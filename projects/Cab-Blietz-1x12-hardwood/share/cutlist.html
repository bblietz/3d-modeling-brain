Assembly

  1. Shell glue‑up

    Finger‑joint the walnut top, bottom, and two sides at the four corners (29 fingers, 9.6 mm, both ends full). Keep the grain wrapping the box: left to right on top and bottom, vertical on the sides, never front to back.

  2. Roundover

    Once the shell is glued and square, rout a 1/2 in (12.7 mm) roundover on every outside edge, front and back perimeters and all four corners.

  3. Baffle

    Cut the birch baffle to fit with 1 mm clearance per side. Bore the 281 mm speaker cutout on the 294.4 mm, 8‑bolt circle and fit the T‑nuts from the back.

  4. Baffle cleats

    Glue and screw the top and bottom cleats only (felt‑lined); no side cleats on this open‑back cab. Hang the baffle on them, 20 mm behind the front edge, removable.

  5. Back cleats and panels

    Install the back cleats (top, bottom, and the four corner pieces), then screw on the upper and lower open‑back panels, 126 mm tall each, about 168 mm open between them. The jack plate cutout goes in the upper panel, 25 mm below the top cleat.

  6. Grill frame

    Half‑lap the four birch strips, wrap and staple the Salt and Pepper cloth, and hang the frame on hook‑and‑loop over the baffle.

  7. Hardware

    Fit the recessed brass jack plate in the upper back panel. Mount the leather strap handle on its two brass end caps, centered on the top panel. Set the four rubber feet. No corners, no piping.

Every dimension here is a design target from the CAD model, not a measurement. Brian measures the walnut and birch stock before cutting the fingers and cleat positions to it.
