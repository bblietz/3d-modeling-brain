Rafter Rod Holder

A printed replacement for the two notched 1x4 combs on your garage joists. Your rods stay put until you pull a neighbour, then any bump drops one. Everything below is drawn to scale from one parameter file; the grams and times are from real slices (PETG, 2 walls, 15% infill, 0.30 mm layers). Pick with the buttons at the bottom.

Why they fall out

A half-round cut into a vertical edge has its lowest point at the edge. The rod rolls down the arc and rests at the mouth with nothing in front of it. A hook pocket puts the floor inside the plate behind a lip, so a rod has to rise over the lip before it can leave.

The comb in place

Same idea as your boards: a plate screwed to the side face of the joist, hanging straight down, rods lying across two of them. It is 2-1/2 in wide and 1/2 in thick, hangs 17 in below the joist (yours hang 20), and holds four rods 4 in apart. The mouths face the same way your notches do.

Decision 1 · Pocket shape

Recommended

A · Hook pocket

5/8 inlift to leave
  • Level mouth: slide the rod in, let it drop.
  • Any blank from 1/4 to 5/8 in sits in the 7/8 in pocket.
  • No moving parts, nothing to relax over the years.

B · Deep hook

1 inlift to leave
  • Mouth higher than the lip: the rod rolls into the pocket by itself.
  • Harder to lift out overhead; each pocket is 3/8 in taller, so the comb grows or the pitch does.
Fit per rod size

C · Snap clip

Gripsevery direction
  • PETG spring fingers close over the blank.
  • Fits 5/16 to 7/16 in blanks only; thinner rods rattle, thicker ones will not go in.
  • Springs relax in a hot garage.

Decision 2 · What gets printed

Recommended

A · Printed comb, three pieces

154 gper comb, 3 h 08 min
pair: 308 g, 6 h 16 min
  • Mount 48 g, module 56 g, end module 51 g; every piece lies flat on the bed.
  • Half-laps with two M4 bolts; the mount takes three #8 x 1-1/2 wood screws.
  • Two rods: mount plus end module, 99 g, hang 9 in. Six rods would hang 25 in, so a second pair instead.
Cheapest fix

B · Caps on your wood combs

20 gper cap, 28 min
your 7 notches: 137 g, 3 h 16 min
  • One cap per notch, 2-3/4 x 3-1/4 x 3/8 in, two #8 screws into the board, mouth 1 in proud of the edge.
  • The boards, their 20 in hang and the 5 in spacing stay as they are.
  • Same hook pocket; the rod ends up inside the old notch.

What I assumed, and what the side view asks

  • Joists 2x8 at 16 in on centre (the 7 in of board above the joist bottom matched a 2x8).
  • Rods per comb 4, like your longer board. Modules add two at a time.
  • Pitch 4 in, first rod 4 in below the joist. If reels stay on and line up, go 5 in or stagger the handles.
  • Blank at the comb 1/4 to 5/8 in; pocket 7/8 in, mouth 1-1/4 in tall.
  • Hang 17 in clears the open door the way your 20 in does.
  • Mouth faces the same way as your notches; say so if it should flip.
  • Screws three #8 x 1-1/2 into the joist, solid pads around every hole in the 3MF.
  • Comb spacing unknown; it only matters for the rods, not the print.
