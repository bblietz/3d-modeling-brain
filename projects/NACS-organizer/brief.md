- `make_plate.py` builds `nacs-organizer-v20-print.3mf` (copy in `~/Downloads/nacs-organizer-v20.3mf`).
- X2D 0.6 nozzle, 0.30mm Standard, Bambu PETG Basic, Textured PEI 70 C.
