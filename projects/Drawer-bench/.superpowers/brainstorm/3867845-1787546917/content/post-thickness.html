How thick should the corner posts be?

Option A locked: proud corner posts. All three fronts below are drawn at the same scale (36" wide x 20" tall) so you can judge visual weight. Post size sets the drawer opening width and the glue-up stock.

A

2-1/2" square

Lighter, more refined. Widest drawer opening (31"). Glue-up: two pieces of 6/4 soft maple, or a single 12/4 blank if you can get it.

B

3" square (recommended)

Solid, bulky proportion for a 20" tall bench without crowding the drawers (30" opening). Cleanest glue-up: two pieces of 8/4 milled to 1-1/2" each.

C

3-1/2" square

Maximum chunk, timber-frame vibe. Costs 2" of drawer width vs A, and needs 10/4 stock or a three-board lamination.

Notes

Recommendation: B. At 20" tall the bench is short and wide; 3" posts read clearly bulky without making the drawers feel pinched. If the final width grows past 40", C starts to make sense. Next up: post details (chamfer/taper, drawer front reveal), then drawer count and layout.
