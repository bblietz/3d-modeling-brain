What the drawing assumes

    
    
      
  • The board hangs with its trucks toward the wall, so the grip side faces the room and the lower wheels rest on the wall. The deck sits off the wall by one truck height plus one wheel radius.
    • 
      
  • The truck is drawn from typical Independent-size numbers (research 2026-10-04, plus or minus 3 mm). Two check values you can measure on your board are in the chips: deck underside to wheel face, and deck underside to the top of the kingpin nut.
    • 
      
  • Concept A carries the weight on the hanger's center body, not on the nut. The nut only stands about 9 mm proud of the hanger's inboard rim and its axis is nearly level, so a hook on the nut alone would hold by friction.
    • 
      
  • Hold is the vertical lift needed to come off, with no friction counted. The final CAD check runs the same test against a mesh built from your measurements.
