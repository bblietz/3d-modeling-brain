# Sharks-nametag

Backpack tag for the kids on the Santa Cruz Sharks soccer team (Santa Cruz City Soccer club). Round tag based on the club logo, one per kid, attached to their backpacks.

## Requirements (from Brian)

- Based on the club logo from the team Instagram: https://www.instagram.com/santa_cruz_city_soccer/
- Remove the "40th anniversary" text from the logo.
- Diameter: 3 inches (76.2 mm). *Assumption:* Brian typed `3'` (feet); 3 ft exceeds the 256 mm build volume and makes no sense for a backpack tag, so interpreting as 3 inches. Flagged for confirmation.

## Assumptions and open questions

- **Attachment:** "attach to their backpacks" implies a hang hole or loop at the top, sized for a ball chain, split ring, or carabiner. Assumed: reinforced hole, ~5 mm inner diameter. Not yet confirmed.
- **Names and numbers:** confirmed 2026-07-31 - each kid's name AND jersey number go on the BACK of the tag; front is the logo. Likely layout: number large and centered, name arced or straight above/below it (final layout at plan time). Model stays parametric so a per-kid batch can be exported. Back-side text style (debossed vs raised) decided at plan time together with print orientation: the back is the bed-side face if the logo prints as an ironed top, and only debossed text prints cleanly on a bed-side face.
- **Colors:** multi-color via AMS 2 Pro, max 4 colors per print. Final palette depends on the reference logo.
- **Nozzle:** rules assume the normally installed 0.6 mm high-flow nozzle (min wall 1.24 mm) unless fine logo detail forces the 0.4 mm.

## Decisions locked (2026-08-01, Brian via question round)

- **Bold simplification:** keep swoosh ring, 4 cyan stars, soccer ball, surfer, SANTA CRUZ banner. Drop SCCYSC, the ~20 tiny side stars, CITY oval, YOUTH SOCCER CLUB arc (all unprintable or marginal at 3").
- **Swoosh stays**, bottom band becomes solid navy where "40th ANNIVERSARY" was.
- **Attachment: hole through the disc** (~5 mm, modeled 5.2 mm) in the white gap at the top; overall size stays exactly 3".
- **Nozzle: 0.4 mm hardened steel** (user will swap it in); min feature ~0.45 mm.
- **Diameter 3 inches = 76.2 mm** (user confirmed the 3' was 3").
- Back carries each kid's **name + jersey number**, debossed, parametric.
- Print orientation: back on bed, logo face up, ironed top; artwork raised ~0.6 mm.
- Colors: white body, navy #00395e, cyan #31bad6 (3 filaments).
