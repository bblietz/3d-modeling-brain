Logo Dude on the cab

The badge artwork on the front of the walnut 1x12 (20 x 18 in, Salt and Pepper cloth), drawn to scale from the CAD geometry. The logo sits in the top-left corner, 10 mm in from the grille edge, the way an amp logo does; say if you want it elsewhere.

  • the 40 mm grille frame strip behind the cloth (the only wood right behind it)
  • speaker cutout and flange (nothing to screw into, and no gap to reach the baffle)
  • cloth to baffle gap behind the open part of the frame: 5 mm

Size

How it screws on (shown on the 3.5 in logo)

AHidden: screwed from behind the frame

Two M3 machine screws come through the 12 mm frame strip and the cloth from the back into brass heat-set inserts in 10 mm bosses on the logo's back (dashed). Nothing shows on the front. Only the band over the frame strip can carry a boss, so the two sit where the logo overlaps it: this far apart on the 3.5 in logo. The grille lifts off with the logo on it (hook-and-loop). Screw spread 93 mm.

BFront screws hidden in the hair

Two #4 black flat-head wood screws countersunk flush into the black hair strokes, through the cloth into the frame strip, drawn true size: the 5.5 mm head is a touch wider than the hair here, so a sliver of head shows on the white. Same band rule: the spots are where the hair crosses the strip. No tools behind the grille. Screw spread 62 mm.

CFront screws to the baffle, anywhere

Same black heads in the hair, but longer screws through the cloth and the 5 mm gap into the baffle, with a 5 mm standoff on the logo's back at each screw so the plate does not crush the cloth. Any spot off the speaker flange works, so the screws can spread wide. The cloth dimples 5 mm under each standoff, hidden under the logo, and the logo stays on when the grille comes off only if the screws are backed out. Screw spread 62 mm.

#4 black flat head, 5.5 mm, flush10 mm boss with an M3 heat-set insert, on the back

My pick: 3.5 in wide with mount A, if you have M3 heat-set inserts (I'll add them to the build if not). It is the classic look, no screw heads, and the grille still comes off as one piece. 3.5 in means the 0.4 nozzle (face lines 1.0 to 1.3 mm, the badge's own recipe); 4 in is the first size the 0.6 that is in the printer handles. B shows a sliver of each head past the hair at this size. C only if you want the logo lower on the grille, off the frame band.

Both A and B leave the chin resting on the cloth with nothing behind it but tension; the plate is stiff, so it should not buzz, and a felt dot under the chin ends it if it does.
