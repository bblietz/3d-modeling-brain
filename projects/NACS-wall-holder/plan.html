NACS Holster Plan

Wall dock for the Tesla Gen 3 Wall Connector handle: nose down, held by a fixed cleat in the connector's own lock notch, with a saddle for the 5.5 m cable. Every drawing is to scale from Tesla's NACS spec and STEP model.

2. How it holds

The nose slides down a socket that fits it to 0.5 mm on three sides. On the wall side sits a fixed 8 mm wide cleat, 2.2 mm proud, placed where the car's lock pin goes. The socket's open side is relieved into a wedge, 7.0 mm wider at the floor, so you can tilt the grip 12° toward the wall and lower the nose past the cleat. Let go and the handle's own weight levers the tip against the wall side (about 1.1 times the handle weight at 25°), which drops the cleat into the notch. The tip rests on the floor; the cleat only blocks a pull straight out.

4. Your calls

Socket angle

25° is drawn. 20° is more compact and holds a little less; 30° sticks out 14 mm more.

Releasing the handle

Tilt and lift is drawn: the square cleat face means a straight pull is blocked, so a bump or the cable's weight cannot unseat it. The alternative is a 45° face on both sides, so a firm straight pull cams the nose out.

One piece or two

One piece, 115 × 240 mm, prints standing up (well inside the 260 mm height) with a brim. Two pieces (socket and saddle on their own plates) print flat and let you put the saddle anywhere, for instance above the Wall Connector.

Saddle

105 mm wide for 6 side-by-side loops of Ø300 mm, 80 mm deep, on four 45° ribs so it prints without support. Say if you coil larger or want it deeper.

5. Build and print

  • build123d from this page's constants; the nose profile comes from Tesla's STEP, so the cavity is the real shape.
  • Material PETG (holds the wedge load, no creep in a warm garage), 0.6 mm high-flow nozzle, 0.30 mm layers, 4 walls, with the Sharks PETG settings.
  • Printed standing on the plate's bottom edge and the socket block: every overhang is under 30°, the saddle rides on its ribs.
  • First print: a 20 mm tall coupon of the socket floor, cleat and notch region to check the fit on the real handle before the full part.
