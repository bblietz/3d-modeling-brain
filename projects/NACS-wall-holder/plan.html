NACS Holster Plan

OpenSCAD model of the wall holder for the Tesla Gen 3 Wall Connector handle: a drum on a square plate, the wand out of the drum's right side at 45° down, hanging on a fixed cleat in the connector's own lock notch. Rendered from holder.scad; the nose profile is Tesla's, from their NACS STEP file.

What is set

  • Plate 120 × 120 × 5 mm, four countersunk holes for #8 screws, 10 mm in from the corners.
  • Drum Ø100, 75 mm out from the plate; teardrop flange Ø124 with its point to the lower left, blended into the drum with an R12 curve (R8 at the plate).
  • Tesla T recessed 1 mm into the flange face, 70 mm tall (57% of the round part, as on the sample), centred on the drum axis, from the official emblem artwork.
  • Wand 45° down and 15° off the wall; the mouth is 36 mm out from the wall, so the wand is hooked in last, outside the cable. The grip clears the wall by about 25 mm where it starts, 36 mm at its middle and 52 mm at its end.
  • Cleat depth: the cleat's holding wall is 31.75 mm (1.25 in) from the opening, measured along the cleat's wall. That seats the whole nose and the glossy housing behind it inside the drum; the cavity's deepest corner comes within 3 mm of the wall, into the 5 mm plate, and the top of the mouth runs about 5 mm up the blend under the flange.
  • Cavity: the connector profile plus 0.5 mm; notch side down, button side up. Cleat 8 mm wide, 2.2 mm proud, on the lower wall, with a 10° hook face so the hanging weight pulls the nose down onto it. Roof relieved 4 mm for insertion, tight over the tip so the handle's weight cannot lever the tip up.
  • Cable: hangs in loops over the top of the drum, as on the sample; 47 mm of straight drum between the two blends, room for three Ø14.5 loops side by side, so the 18 ft cable (about six loops) stacks two deep.

Fit coupon

Only the cavity and cleat, printed the way the holder prints (plate on the bed, tree supports in the cavity roof with Bambu Support For PLA/PETG on the second nozzle). Print time 2 h 13 min, 74 g in all: 68 g PETG and 6 g support material, from a real slice of coupon-print.3mf.

Open

  • Lean: 15°, down from 20°. With the cleat 1.25 in deep the cavity is 62 mm tall inside the drum at 15° and 69 mm at 20°, and the 75 mm drum has 62 mm between the plate and the flange. Keeping 20° would need a drum of about 83 mm.
  • Past the end of Tesla's housing CAD (48 mm from the tip) the grip is not modelled; the cavity there has 1.5 mm extra room and flares. The coupon will show whether your grip clears it.
  • Printing: plate down on the bed. The cavity roof and the ring under the flange need support material (the X2D's support nozzle); everything else prints clean.
