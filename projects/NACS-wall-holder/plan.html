NACS Holster Plan

OpenSCAD model of the wall holder for the Tesla Gen 3 Wall Connector handle: a drum on a square plate, the wand out of the drum's right side at 45° down, hanging on a fixed cleat in the connector's own lock notch. Rendered from holder.scad; the nose profile is Tesla's, from their NACS STEP file.

What is set

  • Plate 120 × 120 × 5 mm, four countersunk holes for #8 screws, 10 mm in from the corners.
  • Drum Ø100, 120 mm out from the plate; teardrop flange teardrop flange Ø124 with its point along the wand#216;124 with its point to the lower left, blended into the drum with an R12 curve (R8 at the plate).
  • Wand 45° down and 20° off the wall; the wand sits at the far end of the drum, 84 mm out from the wall at the mouth, so the cable is wound on the drum behind it and the wand is hooked in last, outside the coil.
  • Cavity: the connector profile plus 0.5 mm; notch side down, button side up. Cleat 8 mm wide, 2.2 mm proud, on the lower wall, with a 10° hook face so the hanging weight pulls the nose down onto it. Roof relieved 4 mm for insertion, tight over the tip so the handle's weight cannot lever the tip up.
  • Cable: wraps the outer 53 mm of drum between the plate and the wand, about three turns per layer; the rest hangs in a loop off the flange, as on the sample.

Open

  • The 20° lean and the 100 mm drum length are my choices; say if the grip should sit further off the wall or you want more cable on the drum.
  • Printing: plate down on the bed. The cavity roof needs support material inside (the X2D's support nozzle); everything else prints clean.
  • Logo on the flange: not yet; a recessed Tesla T from the official artwork if you want it.
  • Next: a coupon print of the cavity and cleat to check the fit on your handle, then the full part.
