NACS Holster Plan

OpenSCAD model of the wall holder for the Tesla Gen 3 Wall Connector handle: a drum on a square plate, the wand out of the drum's right side at 45° down, hanging on a fixed cleat in the connector's own lock notch. Rendered from holder.scad; the nose profile is Tesla's, from their NACS STEP file.

What is set

  • Plate 120 × 120 × 5 mm, four countersunk holes for #8 screws, 10 mm in from the corners.
  • Drum Ø100, 75 mm out from the plate; teardrop flange Ø124 with its point to the lower left, blended into the drum with an R12 curve (R8 at the plate).
  • Tesla T recessed 1 mm into the flange face, 70 mm tall (57% of the round part, as on the sample), centred on the drum axis, from the official emblem artwork.
  • Wand 45° down and 15° off the wall; the mouth is 37 mm out from the wall, so the wand is hooked in last, outside the cable. The grip clears the wall by about 25 mm where it starts, 36 mm at its middle and 52 mm at its end.
  • Cleat depth: the cleat's holding wall is 31.75 mm (1.25 in) from the opening, measured along the cleat's wall. That seats the whole nose and the glossy housing behind it inside the drum; the cavity's deepest corner comes within 3 mm of the wall, into the 5 mm plate, and the top of the mouth runs about 5 mm up the blend under the flange.
  • Cavity: the connector profile plus 0.5 mm; notch side down, button side up. Roof relieved 5 mm for insertion, tight over the tip so the handle's weight cannot lever the tip up.
  • Cleat: a wedge cut to Tesla's lock pocket (spec: 9.71 ±0.2 wide and 6.2 ±0.2 long at its base, 4 ±0.2 deep; in Tesla's CAD it opens to 11.6 wide at the mouth and has a domed base). 10.6 mm wide at the floor narrowing to 9.3, 3.57 mm tall, ramp at 31° from the mouth side up to a sharp edge at the back, back face overhanging 15°. The nose stops 2.5 mm short of the cavity's end wall when hanging; that is the travel for pushing the pocket past the edge so the nose drops onto the cleat.
  • Cable: hangs in loops over the top of the drum, as on the sample; 47 mm of straight drum between the two blends, room for three Ø14.5 loops side by side, so the 18 ft cable (about six loops) stacks two deep.

Cleat size: 50% longer, asked 2026-09-18

Tesla's drawing and CAD give a lock pocket 6.5 mm long at its mouth and 4.0 mm deep. The cleat as modelled already fills it to within 0.45 mm. Half as long again at the same angle is also half as tall again, and that does not go into the pocket in Tesla's CAD: the wand would sit on top of the wedge and not latch.

Both are ready to print. coupon-print.3mf has the cleat that fits Tesla's pocket. coupon-long-print.3mf has the 50% longer one, 1 h 34 min and 56 g. A caliper on the wand's pocket settles which: if it measures near 6.5 long and 4 deep, only the first can work.

Fit coupon

The coupon is the holder's nose cavity and the new cleat, cut off just past the nose shoulder, printed the way the holder prints (plate on the bed, tree supports in the cavity roof with Bambu Support For PLA/PETG on the second nozzle). Print time 1 h 29 min, 54 g in all: 50 g PETG and 4 g support material, from a real slice of coupon-print.3mf.

It proves the nose profile and its 0.5 mm clearance, the cleat in the lock pocket, the tight roof over the tip, and lifting the nose over the cleat. The deeper opening gets no coupon of its own (Brian, 2026-09-17) and is first tried on the full part.

Open

  • Lean: 15°, down from 20°. With the cleat 1.25 in deep the cavity is 62 mm tall inside the drum at 15° and 69 mm at 20°, and the 75 mm drum has 62 mm between the plate and the flange. Keeping 20° would need a drum of about 83 mm.
  • Past the end of Tesla's housing CAD (48 mm from the tip) the grip is not modelled; the cavity there has 1.5 mm extra room and flares. The coupon will show whether your grip clears it.
  • Printing: plate down on the bed. The cavity roof and the ring under the flange need support material (the X2D's support nozzle); everything else prints clean.
  • Next: print the coupon, try the handle on it (insertion over the cleat, hanging, lift-off), then the full part with the deep opening.
