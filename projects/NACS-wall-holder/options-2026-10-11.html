The drum runs past the 4 in base

Brian, October 11: the center cylinder extends past the edge of the base on all 4 sides. True, and it dates from the October 8 revision, not the plate fix: the drum went from 3-1/8 to 3-9/16 in across so the deeper cavity would fit, and the drum's blend lands 9/32 in outside it. The blend's foot is now 2-3/64 in from the axis and the plate's edge 2 in, so at the middle of each side the blend runs 3/64 in off the edge, and the flange (the drum plus its blend, 4-3/32 in across) is 3/32 in wider than the plate. The September print had a smaller drum and the foot sat 1/8 in inside the edge.

As built

Every option below keeps the screw pattern (holes 3-7/32 in apart), so the anchors already in the wall are reused. Print cost is estimated from the plate's extra plastic; the real slice follows the pick.

C: base stays 4 in, smaller blends

The drum keeps its size but both blends shrink from 9/32 to 5/32 in, so the foot lands 1/64 in inside the plate's flat top. The flange is the drum plus its blend, so the face shrinks with it: 3-7/8 in across instead of 4-3/32, the lip the same, the T a touch smaller. The blends at both ends are tighter curves. No extra cost.
