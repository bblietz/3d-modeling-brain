Leader card LC2

The hook slots become straight tapered slots, and both wavy wrap edges become a comb of the same slots. Every slot is 3/8 in deep and tapers from a 1.2 mm mouth to closed, so 10 lb, 25 lb and 40 lb line each wedge at their own depth.

One slot, close up

Long-edge slots and comb slots share this exact shape. The taper is 7.2° overall, so each line size stops well apart from the next, and the thinnest 10 lb line still wedges before the slot closes.

What changes from LC1

Removed

  • Hook bend and its 18 mm entry channel
  • Entry funnel at the slot mouth
  • Exit ramps on both faces
  • Wavy wrap edges
  • Raised corner lugs

New

  • Straight tapered slot on each long edge
  • Comb of tapered slots on both wrap edges
  • Width 1.5 in (was 2 in)
  • Label bumped to LC2

Kept

  • 3 in long, 3 mm thick, white PETG
  • 0.6 mm round and chamfer on every slot face edge
  • Rounded slot mouths and tooth tips

Before I build it

  1. "Remove the lead-in": this drawing drops the exit ramps as well as the entry channel. Keep the ramps instead?
  2. "1/3 of the way from the wrap edge": drawn as 1 in from the wrap edge, one slot on each long edge at opposite ends.as drawn
  3. Comb: 7 slots per edge, 4 mm apart, as many as fit the 1.5 in edge between the label strips. Tighter spacing gives more slots but thinner teeth.as drawn
  4. Corner lugs: dropped, since the comb slots already stop the line sliding off.as drawn
