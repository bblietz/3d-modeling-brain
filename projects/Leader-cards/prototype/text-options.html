Leader Card wordmark

Four ways to add a recessed "Leader" / "Card" wordmark to the LC2 card's open interior, one word per line. The small "LC2" label stays put in its own strip for field-test reference.

The interior between the two wrap-edge combs is exactly 2.25 in (57.15 mm) long — the card's 3 in length minus a 3/8 in comb depth off each end. Every option below sizes the wordmark to fill that same span, so this isn't an arbitrary width: it's the full clear area the combs leave behind.

1

Even weight

“Leader” is sized to fill the full 2.25 in interior; “Card” sits under it at the same letter height, so it comes out narrower. Same bold face as the LC2 label.

DejaVu Sans Bold · “Leader” 15.1 mm cap size, “Card” 15.1 mm · block 27.5 mm tall · fills the full 2.25 in

2

Poster fill

Both words are scaled independently to each fill the full 2.25 in, so “Card”’s four letters come out taller than “Leader”’s six. A bolder, more graphic wordmark.

DejaVu Sans Bold · “Leader” 13.4 mm cap size, “Card” 20.4 mm · block 30.0 mm tall · fills 1.99 in of the 2.25 in target (shrunk to clear the middle side slot and the LC2 label)

3

Condensed caps

Same even-weight rule as the first option, but a condensed, all-caps face. The narrower letterforms leave more vertical room, so the block sits smaller in the band.

Liberation Sans Narrow Bold · “Leader” 14.0 mm cap size, “Card” 14.0 mm · block 23.0 mm tall · fills 1.82 in of the 2.25 in target (shrunk to clear the middle side slot and the LC2 label)

4

Tight, maximal scale

Same face and sizing rule as the first option, but the lines sit almost touching, using the freed-up height to read as one bold block from across the tackle box.

DejaVu Sans Bold · “Leader” 15.1 mm cap size, “Card” 15.1 mm · block 24.3 mm tall · fills the full 2.25 in
