Leader card LC2

Both wrap edges are a comb of straight tapered slots, and one long edge has three more at 25, 50 and 75 percent of the length for starting and finishing the leader. Every slot is 3/8 in deep and tapers from a 1.2 mm mouth to closed, so 10 lb, 25 lb and 40 lb line each wedge at their own depth.

Comb slots, close up

Every comb slot has this exact shape. The taper is 7.2° overall, so each line size stops well apart from the next, and the thinnest 10 lb line still wedges before the slot closes.

What changes from LC1

Removed

  • Hook bend and its 18 mm entry channel
  • Hook slots at the long-edge corners
  • Entry funnel at the slot mouth
  • Exit ramps on both faces
  • Wavy wrap edges
  • Raised corner lugs

New

  • Comb of 9 tapered slots on each wrap edge
  • 3 side slots on one long edge at 25, 50 and 75%
  • Width 1.5 in (was 2 in)
  • Label bumped to LC2

Kept

  • 3 in long, 3 mm thick, white PETG
  • 0.6 mm round and chamfer on every slot face edge
  • Rounded slot mouths and tooth tips

Decided for LC2

  • Exit ramps removed along with the entry channel and funnel.
  • Three side slots on the long edge opposite the label, at 25, 50 and 75% of the length.
  • Tighter comb: 9 slots per wrap edge, 3 mm apart.
  • Raised corners dropped; the comb slots stop the line sliding off.
