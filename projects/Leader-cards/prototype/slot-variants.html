Leader card slots

You have not picked a slot yet. The card in the 3D viewer shows variant 1 only as a stand-in. Variants 1 to 4 are the ones on the test coupon, in order from its left end; variant 5 is a new option. The winner goes on the card after you test the coupon with real 25 lb and 40 lb line.

Reading a drawing

Each drawing is the corner of the card around one slot, seen from above and drawn to scale from the CAD model. The line presses in from the long edge, slides around the hook, and wedges in the pocket. Wrap tension pulls it toward the wavy wrap edge on the right, up and out along the exit ramp.

What to decide

Pick which variants go on the test coupon: keep the four, swap one for variant 5, or add variant 5 as a fifth slot. Every variant gets the same rounded edges and exit ramps. Line stop positions assume the pocket prints to its drawn width; if the printed pocket comes out a little narrower, each line simply stops a little closer to the corner.
