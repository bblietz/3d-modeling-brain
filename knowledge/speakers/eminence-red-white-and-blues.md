# Eminence Red, White and Blues 12

38 oz ceramic magnet, 1.75 in coil. 8 ohm only.

## Character

- low_end: tight. mids: neutral. top: chimey. breakup: clean.
- Eminence: "Nice tight low-end, smooth midrange and top end sparkle. American cousin to The Governor."

## Best with

- Amp families: blackface Fender, modeling and solid state, boutique clean. Genres: funk and R&B, worship and pop, roots and country.
