# WGS Green Beret

WGS's 25 W Greenback style speaker.

## Character

- low_end: balanced. mids: forward. top: smooth. breakup: early.
- WGS: "brings crunchy cone breakup, growling bass, great headroom, and aggressive mids together better than any other 25-watt speaker."

## Best with

- Amp families: Marshall, Vox, tweed Fender. Genres: classic rock, blues. Qts 1.18 as printed: prefers open or semi-open backs; two per cabinet for amps over 20 W.

## Data notes

- WGS prints: Fs 125.3, Re 8.05, Le 0.34, Qes 1.35, Qms 9.52, Qts 1.18, Vas "10.5 cu ft", Sd 366.1, Mms 29.2, BL 11.7, SPL 99.63 dB, mounting as the ET65.
- Assumptions: Vas taken as 10.5 L; Sd 530 cm2; Xmax 0.8 mm; magnet ceramic. The printed Fs of 125 Hz is high for a Greenback style speaker (Celestion's G12M reads 75 Hz) and may be a new-unit measurement. Estimates.
