# Celestion G12M Greenback (25 W)

## Character

- low_end: balanced. mids: forward. top: smooth. breakup: early.
- Celestion: "This model is voiced with additional broad mid-range attack and restrained top-end to give a forward punchy attitude to chords and a searing lead tone without fizz."

## Best with

- Amp families: Marshall, Vox, Tweed Fender. Genres: Classic rock; Blues; Indie and alternative. Two per cabinet under amps over 20 W. Families and genres per [[speaker-cab-voicing]].
