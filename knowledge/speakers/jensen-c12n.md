# Jensen C12N

Vintage ceramic, 50 W (100 W musical), 38 mm coil, 810 g ferrite magnet.

## Character

- low_end: balanced. mids: neutral. top: chimey. breakup: moderate.
- Jensen: "Smooth and straightforward, full bodied tone with high frequency chime. It responds to overdrive distortion with powerful mid bite."

## Best with

- Amp families: Blackface Fender, Tweed Fender. Genres: Roots, country, alt-country; Jazz; Blues. Qts above 1: prefers open or semi-open; closed boxes under about 59 L net come out peaky (Qtc 1.26 at 44 L; 1.18, big, at the 68 L clamp). Families and genres per [[speaker-cab-voicing]].
