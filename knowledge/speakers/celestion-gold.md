# Celestion Gold

## Character

- low_end: balanced. mids: forward. top: chimey. breakup: moderate.
- Celestion: "Building on the platform of the Blue the Gold is a higher-powered Alnico-magnet guitar speaker that recreates the unmistakable sonic signature of a 'well played-in' original."
