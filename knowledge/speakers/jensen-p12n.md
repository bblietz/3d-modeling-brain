# Jensen P12N

Vintage alnico, 50 W (100 W musical), 38 mm coil, 826 g alnico magnet.

## Character

- low_end: balanced. mids: forward. top: chimey. breakup: moderate.
- Jensen: "Very warm and full bodied tone with powerful mid-bite and sparkling highs. When presented with overdrive distortion it displays a bright and colorful crunch."

## Best with

- Amp families: Tweed Fender, Blackface Fender. Genres: Blues; Roots, country, alt-country; Jazz. Historically an open-back combo speaker; prefers open or semi-open. Families and genres per [[speaker-cab-voicing]].

## Data notes

- 8 ohm T/S from the Jensen spec sheet: Fs 90, Re 6.03, Le 0.87, Qes 0.94, Qms 4.36, Qts 0.77, Vas 34.6 L, Sd 490.9, Xmax 1.0, Mms 30.9 g, BL 10.62. 16 ohm: Fs 91, Re 12, Qts 0.84, Vas 42.2 L, 97.8 dB.
