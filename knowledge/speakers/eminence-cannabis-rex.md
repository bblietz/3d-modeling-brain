# Eminence Cannabis Rex 12

Hemp cone, 38 oz ceramic magnet, 1.75 in coil. On the MaximoCabs tolex line list.

## Character

- low_end: balanced. mids: neutral. top: smooth. breakup: moderate.
- Eminence: "Clean and full, with lots of body and sparkle. Smokey smooth with high-end definition."

## Best with

- Amp families: Blackface Fender, Boutique clean, Tweed Fender.
- Genres: roots-country, jazz, indie-alternative, blues
