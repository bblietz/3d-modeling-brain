# Celestion Heritage G12-65

## Character

- low_end: tight. mids: forward. top: smooth. breakup: moderate.
- Celestion: "the G12-65 is widely regarded as one of the best guitar speakers ever produced for use in a 4x12 cabinet."

## Best with

- Amp families: Marshall, modern high gain at moderate gain. Genres: classic rock, blues.
